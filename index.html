Before you book.

The questions we are asked most, answered.

When and where is Navkar Navratri Utsav 2026?

Nine nights, 11 to 19 October 2026, at Jalavihar, Necklace Road, Hyderabad. Gates open at 7:00 PM every night.

How do I book my passes?

Tap Book Your Passes, pick your night, choose your tickets, then pay on Razorpay. The Navkar team verifies every payment manually. Single night entry is ₹499, children aged 3 to 5 are ₹299 for one night or ₹1,500 for a Kids Season Pass (all nine days, food not included), and a Season Pass covering all nine nights is ₹2,000.

I have never done Garba. Can I still join?

Yes, and plenty of people arrive exactly that way. Every night is beginner friendly — experienced dancers and first-timers share the same circle.

Is there food, and are the premises alcohol-free?

There is a food court on site with a range of festive options to refuel between rounds. The premises are strictly alcohol-free, with professional security across all nine nights, so families and first-timers are as comfortable as the regulars.

Can I book for a large group or a family?

Yes. There are ready-made Group of 4 and Group of 10 passes, and for anything larger the bulk desk will sort it out with you directly on WhatsApp.
